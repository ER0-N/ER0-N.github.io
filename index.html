About Me

I'm Eron — a 20-year-old Computer Science student based in Erbil, Kurdistan, currently in my second stage. I'm drawn to the whole spectrum of building software: development, AI, databases, web and app development, and Linux.

What defines how I work is that I'm self-taught and hands-on. I'd rather build a real application than only study the theory behind it — so I spend my time shipping small tools, deploying bots to my own servers, and tearing into how things actually run under the hood.

Outside the terminal, two things fuel me: BMWs and the gym. Together with code, they became my personal brand — Torque Byte Fit.
